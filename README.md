Device tree for the Realme 7 (RMX2151, RMX2153, RMX2155 and RMX2163) - Realme Narzo 20 Pro/Narzo 30 4G (RMX2161, RMX2156)

The Realme 7 (codenamed _"RMX2151, RMX2153, RMX2155 and RMX2163"_) - Realme Narzo 20 Pro/Narzo 30 4G (codenamed _"RMX2161, RMX2156"_) are mid-range smartphones from Realme.
They were released in September 2020 and May 2021.
